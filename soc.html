Summary

I'm a Cybersecurity undergraduate at Multimedia University, passionate about building secure, real-world systems from full-stack web applications to intelligent, data-driven platforms. My work sits at the intersection of software development and security.

My projects reflect that range. I built GovCare+, a multilingual government complaint routing system powered by NLP (BERT). Beyond development, I actively participate in CTF competitions and security workshops covering OSINT, web exploitation, reverse engineering, cryptography, and digital forensics. I work with tools like Kali Linux, Wireshark and OWASP ZAP, driven by the mindset that good software isn't just built, it's built to withstand the unexpected.
